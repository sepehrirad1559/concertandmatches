Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ConcertAndMatches.com ("ConcertAndMatches," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our site.

1. Information We Collect

If you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text). If you browse without an account, we don't require any personal information to search or view events.

Like most websites, we automatically collect some technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages viewed — through standard web server logs and, where enabled, analytics tools.

2. Affiliate Links and Tracking

ConcertAndMatches earns commissions through affiliate partnerships with ticket sellers (Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, StubHub, Vivid Seats, and similar). When you click a "Find Tickets" link, you're redirected through a tracking link operated by our affiliate network (such as Impact.com or Partnerize) so the partner can attribute the referral to us. This tracking link may set a cookie or pass a click identifier to the partner's site — it does not give us access to anything you do after you land on the partner's site, including your payment information or the details of any purchase you make there.

Once you leave ConcertAndMatches and land on a partner's website, that site's own privacy policy applies to any information you provide there.

3. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality (like keeping you logged in) and, if analytics are enabled, to understand how visitors use the Site in aggregate. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Site, maintain your account if you have one, understand aggregate usage patterns, and measure the performance of our affiliate links (e.g., how many clicks a partner link received).

5. How We Share Information

We don't sell your personal information. We share information only: (a) with affiliate networks and partners as needed for click tracking and commission attribution, described above; (b) with service providers who help us run the Site (such as hosting and database providers); or (c) if required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active, and technical/log data for a limited period for security and troubleshooting purposes.

7. Your Choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us at the email below. You can also decline cookies through your browser, though some site features may not work as intended.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Site after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
